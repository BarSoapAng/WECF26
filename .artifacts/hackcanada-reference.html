About Hack Canada

Hack Canada is a hackathon that brings together some of the most talented hackers from universities across Canada. We partner with student clubs across the country and offer travel reimbursement to make it easier for students from different campuses to join us.

We’re here to showcase Canada’s tech ecosystem, connect people who love building things, and give them the space, community, and support to create some of their coolest projects yet.

Wallhacks

Made by:Zane Beeai · JaxonKaz KazakeviciusSebastian Arellano-Rubach · Milind Maheshwari

Wallhacks uses five radar sensors to detect people behind non-metal obstacles. AR headsets show their positions as live markers and a confidence heatmap, with multiple users sharing the same view.

Ember

Made by:Taimoor Aleem · George FaragRita Zhang · Yves Donato

Ember is a firefighting prototype with AR navigation and object detection for smoke-filled spaces. Its command dashboard streams the responder’s camera view and displays biometric data to help commanders follow conditions inside.
